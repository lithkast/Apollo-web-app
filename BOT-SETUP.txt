Hermes the bot: setting him up
==============================

Hermes posts each Calendar event in your Discord: an announcement at 10:00 AM Eastern on the day of the event, and a
"we're open" post when the doors open. Owners, co-owners and the administrator write both posts on the site.

A Discord bot is an account for a program. You make it once in Discord's Developer Portal, give its password (the
"token") to Firebase, and invite it to your server. It takes about 15 minutes, and you never need to touch code.


PART 1: MAKE THE BOT IN DISCORD
-------------------------------

Step 1. Create Hermes
   Open https://discord.com/developers/applications and sign in with your Discord account.
   Click New Application (top right), name it Hermes, tick the box to agree, and click Create.

Step 2. His name and picture
   In the menu on the left, click Bot.
   Under Icon, upload Hermes' picture. Make sure Username says Hermes. Click Save Changes.

Step 3. Let him look people up
   Still on the Bot page, scroll down to Privileged Gateway Intents.
   Switch on SERVER MEMBERS INTENT and click Save Changes.
   (This is what lets the Mention button on the site find people by name. Leave the other two off.)

Step 4. Keep him yours (optional, recommended)
   So nobody else can add Hermes to their server:
     a. In the left menu, click Installation. Set Install Link to None and click Save Changes.
     b. Go back to Bot and switch off Public Bot. Save Changes.
   (Do a. before b., or Discord shows an error.)


PART 2: GET FIREBASE READY
--------------------------

Step 5. Switch on Storage (for the pictures and videos in the posts)
   Open https://console.firebase.google.com and pick ApolloHR.
   In the left menu: Build > Storage > Get started.
     - For the location, pick one marked as no-cost, such as US-CENTRAL1. It can't be changed later.
     - Start in production mode. (The deploy replaces those rules with the site's own.)
   Click Done (or Create). You only ever do this once.

Step 6. Give Hermes' token to Firebase
   a. Back in the Developer Portal, on the Bot page, click Reset Token, confirm, and click Copy.
      (Discord may ask for your password or 2FA code first.)
      The token is Hermes' password. Never paste it in Discord, on the site, or anywhere it can be seen on stream.
   b. Open a terminal in the site folder (the one with firebase.json in it) and run:

         firebase functions:secrets:set DISCORD_BOT_TOKEN

      When it says "Enter a value for DISCORD_BOT_TOKEN", paste the token (right-click or Ctrl+V) and press Enter.
      Nothing shows on screen while you paste: that is on purpose.
      If it asks to switch on Secret Manager, say yes.
   The token is kept in Google's Secret Manager. It is never in the site, the database, or these files.

   If you ever need a new token (it leaked, or the site says Discord doesn't accept it): do this step again, then
   run firebase deploy.

Step 7. Deploy
   In the same terminal, run:

         firebase deploy

     - "Cloud Storage for Firebase needs an IAM Role to use cross-service rules. Grant the new role?": type y.
       (This lets the storage rules check who is an owner.)
     - If it asks for DISCORD_BOT_TOKEN, you skipped step 6: paste the token now.
     - If it asks to switch on Cloud Scheduler or other Google services, say yes. (Hermes checks every minute
       for posts that are due.)
     - The first time takes several minutes. If it says to try again in a few minutes, wait and run it again.


PART 3: PUT HERMES IN YOUR SERVER
---------------------------------

Step 8. Invite him and pick his channel
   On https://apolloncc.org, signed in as the administrator, open Settings > Discord. Under "Hermes the bot":
     a. Click "Invite Hermes to your server". Discord opens: pick Apollo Night Club & Casino, click Continue,
        leave every permission ticked, and click Authorize.
     b. Back on the site, click Check again. It should say Hermes is connected.
     c. Under "Channel for event posts", pick the channel the announcements go in.

   If that channel is locked so only staff can post, let Hermes in too: in Discord, Edit Channel > Permissions >
   add Hermes, and allow View Channel, Send Messages, Embed Links, Attach Files and Mention @everyone.

Step 9. Try it
   Tip: for a first try, pick a quiet test channel in step 8c, and switch it back afterwards.
   Calendar > click a day with an event > click "Discord posts" next to the event.
     - Each event starts from the house templates (your usual APOLLO PRESENTS post). Change anything you like.
     - The preview underneath shows how it will look in Discord.
     - Click "Schedule it" on each post you want sent. Nothing is ever posted before you do.


PART 4: CLOCKING IN AND OUT FROM DISCORD
----------------------------------------
Once Hermes is in your server (Part 3), he can also clock people in and out on the HR page with slash commands:
/clockin, /clockout, /pfclockin and /pfclockout (party finders and shouts), /shift (your hours and pay so far tonight),
/onshift (who is on the clock), /hrlink and /help. (Linking is /hrlink, not /link, because other
bots, like the birthday bot, already have a /link.)

Step 10. Deploy, then give Discord Hermes' address
   a. Run firebase deploy as usual.
   b. On https://apolloncc.org, open Settings > Discord. Under "Clocking in with Hermes", click Copy next to
      the Interactions Endpoint URL.
   c. In the Developer Portal, open Hermes, then General Information. Paste it into INTERACTIONS ENDPOINT URL and
      click Save Changes. Discord checks the address straight away. If it says it could not verify it, wait a
      minute (the new functions may still be starting) and save again.
   d. Within a minute, typing / in your server shows Hermes' commands. If they don't show, click "Set up the
      commands again" in Settings > Discord.

Step 11. Keep the commands to one channel (optional)
   In Discord: Server Settings > Integrations > Hermes. There you can pick which channels and roles can use each
   command, for example only a #clock-in channel.

Step 12. Everyone links their Discord, once
   Each person's login must be tied to their row on the HR roster (Account management > Logins and permissions, on
   your profile page, as for pay).
   Then they open their profile on the site, click "Get a link code" in the Discord box (only they see it), and type
   /hrlink in the server with that code. The code works once, for 10 minutes. After that, their Discord and their login
   belong together until they unlink it from that same box (or you do, from their card in Staff Profiles), so nobody
   can clock in as someone else.

How the clock commands work
- Every reply from Hermes is private: only the person who typed the command sees it.
- /clockin and /clockout stamp the time like the tick boxes on the HR page do, rounded to the quarter hour.
- Owners and co-owners can fix someone else's clock: /clockin member:@someone (or /clockout).
- Clocking in more than an hour before doors open, clocking in after closing, clocking out more than half an hour
  after closing, and every fix by an owner show under "To review" on the HR page, for owners and co-owners. You can
  change the hour and the half hour in Settings > Discord. On a night with a Calendar event, its times count.
- Someone on LOA can't clock themselves in, and nobody can while the site is in maintenance mode.

Party finders and shouts (promoting Apollo)
- /pfclockin asks what they're doing, with three buttons: Party finder, Shouts or Both. Pressing one clocks them in for
  it, to the quarter hour. /pfclockout clocks them out, and the time goes on their row of the HR page.
- It is paid by the hour on top of their shift: 60,000g for party finders and 140,000g for shouts (both at once earns
  both). Change the rates in Settings > Promotion pay.
- On the HR page, the Promotion hours column shows each person's hours (you can type them too), and anyone still
  promoting, with an End button. Pay includes it, and it is logged to Data with the night and counted on profiles.
  Start a new night clears it. Promoting counts as showing up, so it adds no missed day.
- Owners and co-owners can add member:@someone to /pfclockin and /pfclockout too; those show under "To review".
- If the new commands don't show in Discord a minute after you deploy, click "Set up the commands again" in
  Settings > Discord.


HOW IT WORKS
------------
- The morning announcement goes out at 10:00 AM Eastern on the day of the event. The doors-open post goes out when
  the event starts. Either time can be changed for one event with "Posts at" (and put back with "Usual time").
- Your changes save by themselves as you type. If a post is already in Discord, Hermes edits it there within a
  minute. Changing its picture or video posts it again (Discord can't swap an attachment), and switching
  "Post this" off takes it down.
- Changing the event on the Calendar (its name, day or times) updates the posts and the Discord Event too.
- Words in {curly brackets} are filled in when Hermes posts: {event} the event's name, {start} its date and time,
  {end} the closing time, {countdown} how long until it starts, {where} and {details}. Times show on each reader's
  own clock in Discord.
- Emoji lists your server's own emojis. Mention finds people by name, and has your roles.
- "Also list it in the server's Events tab" adds the event to Discord's Events list as well (on by default).
- "Save these as the house templates" makes this event's posts the starting point for new events.
- Only owners, co-owners and you can see or change the posts. In maintenance mode, only you.


IF SOMETHING SAYS...
--------------------
"Hermes isn't allowed to post there"    Give him the permissions in step 8 on that channel, then Try again.
"Deleted in Discord"                    Someone deleted the post. "Post it again" sends it once more.
"Hermes can't look people up yet"       Switch on Server Members Intent (step 3).
"Discord does not accept the token"     Get a new token and set it again (step 6), then firebase deploy.
"Hermes' functions can't be reached"    Run firebase deploy (step 7).


WHAT IT COSTS
-------------
Nothing in practice. The every-minute check, the posts and the files all fit well inside the Blaze plan's free
allowances (Cloud Scheduler gives 3 free jobs and Hermes uses 1). Pictures and videos are deleted when their event
is deleted from the Calendar.
